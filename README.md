# BookNook

**A small neighborhood library on the web.** BookNook helps readers share books from their shelves and discover books they can borrow from others.

## Live website

Visit BookNook: [https://booknook-frontend-orpin.vercel.app/](https://booknook-frontend-orpin.vercel.app/)

## Features

- **Browse the community shelf** to explore books shared by readers.
- **Add books to the library** with details such as title, author, category, copy count, cover image, and description.
- **Discover books at a glance** with a responsive card layout that highlights each book’s cover and availability.
- **View book details** to learn more about a title before borrowing.
- **Track your own books** in a personal collection.
- **Keep up with borrowing activity** through a dedicated borrows section.
- **Enjoy a responsive experience** designed to work across desktop and mobile screens.

## About

BookNook brings the neighborhood bookshelf online. Share a book you have enjoyed, find your next read, and help good stories travel from one reader to another.
